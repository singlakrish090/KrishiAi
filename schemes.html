INCOME SUPPORT

PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Provides eligible farmer families with financial assistance of ₹6,000 per year, paid in three instalments subject to scheme rules.

Who may be eligible?

Eligible landholding farmer families, subject to applicable exclusions and verification requirements. eKYC is required under the scheme.

CROP INSURANCE

PM Fasal Bima Yojana

A government crop insurance scheme intended to help eligible farmers manage specified crop-loss risks.

Who may be eligible?

Eligibility, notified crops, insurance units and enrolment periods depend on the applicable season and state notifications.

Provides eligible farmers access to agricultural credit for qualifying farming and related expenses through participating financial institutions.
